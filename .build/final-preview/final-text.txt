                                                                         Problem Definition
PROJECT FINAL EXAMINATION




                Street-vendor allocation combines policy, capacity, livelihood and spatial constraints.


                                                  Fragmented records                                                 Manual decisions
                       Vendor, zone, document and allocation data are difficult to reconcile.     Static lists do not explain why one zone is preferable to another.




                                                      Live constraints                                              Spatial uncertainty
                    Capacity, status and relocation rules can change after a recommendation.    Reference coordinates are analytical evidence, not legal boundaries.




                                Decision-support goal: make evidence visible while keeping final municipal authority with the officer.

        Authorization                                                     Server-enforced VENDOR, ONBOARDING and ADMIN roles          UI visibility alone never grants access

                                                           Limitations and Responsible Use
PROJECT FINAL EXAMINATION




                                                      Spatial authority                                                              Outcome evidence
                          Analytical reference points are not certified vending boundaries.                      No verified footfall, revenue, safety or displacement outcomes exist.




                                                      Model meaning                                                                     Field validation
                            K-Means is descriptive; MCDA sensitivity is scenario-specific.                            Pilot slots lack obstruction survey and physical approval.




                                                      External services                                                               Operational scale
                    Production OTP, WhatsApp, routing and AI need live-provider validation.               Load, accessibility, backup recovery and long-term monitoring need formal testing.




                                                  Final allocation remains a municipal decision supported by evidence, policy and field inspection.
