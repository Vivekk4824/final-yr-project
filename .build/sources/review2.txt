Future Scope
1. Real-Time Municipal Integration
    Integrate NMC data for live zone capacity, availability and vendor status.
    Automatically update recommendations as conditions change.
2. Advanced Recommendation Model
    Use expert-validated suitability labels to develop supervised ML models.
    Improve recommendation accuracy through continuous feedback.
3. WhatsApp-Based Vendor Assistance
    Provide zone recommendations and zone information through WhatsApp.
    Enable easier access through multilingual interaction.
4. What-If GIS Analysis
    Allow users to modify factors such as accessibility, capacity and commercial activity.
    Instantly observe their impact on zone recommendations.
