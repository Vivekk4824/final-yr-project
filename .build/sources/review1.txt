Problem Definition
                                                          Current System :
Street vendor management in many urban areas is
largely manual, making vendor management, zoning, and
allocation inefficient for municipal authorities. The
existing system lacks a centralized and data-driven
approach for vendor allocation, zoning, and monitoring.


Key Challenges
   Manual registration and license management.
   Time-consuming search and record updates.
   Manual vendor allocation within designated zones.
   Lack of data-driven zoning and allocation
   decisions.
   Uneven vendor distribution, leading to traffic
   congestion and poor space utilization.
   Limited visibility of vendor distribution and zone
   occupancy.
Scope

The project focuses on developing a GIS-Enabled AI-Assisted Street
Vendor Zoning and Management System for urban areas. The system
will:

   Digital vendor registration and profile management.
   Unique vendor licensing.
   Vendor allocation within PMC-designated vending zones.
   AI-based location suitability analysis.
   GIS-based zoning and map visualization.
   Vendor and zone monitoring dashboard.
Objectives


   Develop a web-based street vendor management platform.
   Implement digital vendor registration and unique licensing.
   Build a centralized GIS-enabled vendor and zoning database.
   Develop a data-driven recommendation engine for vendor
   allocation within designated vending zones.
   Evaluate location suitability using spatial and urban factors such
   as vendor density, road network, nearby facilities, accessibility, and
   traffic conditions.
   Visualize recommended allocations and zone occupancy through an
   interactive GIS dashboard.
   Provide a decision support system to assist municipal authorities
   in vendor allocation and zoning decisions.
