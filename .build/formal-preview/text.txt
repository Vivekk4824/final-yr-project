Problem Definition
PROJECT FINAL EXAMINATION




       - Municipal vendor records, zones and spatial evidence are
       fragmented.
       - Manual allocation is difficult to explain, audit and update.
       - Zone suitability depends on policy, capacity, access and
       livelihood factors.
       - Final decisions require both analytical evidence and officer
       control.

Project Objectives
PROJECT FINAL EXAMINATION




       - Prepare traceable NMC and OpenStreetMap spatial datasets.
       - Profile vending environments through unsupervised learning.
       - Rank eligible zones through explainable multi-criteria analysis.
       - Support vendor requests, officer review, capacity and audit
       workflows.
       - Provide multilingual web and WhatsApp access.

K-Means Environmental Profiling
PROJECT FINAL EXAMINATION




       - Input: 258 usable zone references.
       - Features: 12 scaled environmental variables.
       - Model selection compared candidate K values.
       - Selected K = 2 with five-seed stability testing.
       - Purpose: descriptive typology, not suitability prediction.

MCDA Recommendation Method
PROJECT FINAL EXAMINATION




                 Score(z) = SUM [ weight(i) x normalized factor(i,z) ]

         - Eligibility filters are applied before scoring.
         - Each factor contribution is shown to the user.
         - Missing values are handled without inventing evidence.
         - Officer approval is required before allocation.




Department of Information Technology | A.Y. 2026-27                      22
MCDA Parameters and Baseline Weights
PROJECT FINAL EXAMINATION



                 - Business compatibility - 25%
                 - Commercial activity - 20%
                 - Access - 20%
                 - Facilities - 15%
                 - Vendor preference - 10%
                 - Historical evidence - 10%
